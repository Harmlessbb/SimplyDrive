# SimplyDrive

Simply Drive is an Application that intends to be used as a bridge between customers looking to get their cars serviced, diagnosed, repaired, and MOT tested.

It hopes to achieve maximum convenience for both the service advisors at the dealership and the customers, who will be able to have the ability to make a booking at
the click of a button, instead of having to wait hours at a time for a phone call. 

This includes the function of a Live calendar, to make sure bookings are accurate, to avoid customers turning up at places that are overbooked.

One Key Part of the whole application is a sub-application called "Artificial Service Advisor", from this point forward it will be called the ASA for short, this handles
the actual requests and does the role that a service advisor would do in real life, I.E update the calendar, write the physical job cards, etc. The ASA will need direct access to the dealerships document manager, this will be solved once a dealership has agreed to rolling out a test phase, as we don't currently have access to a real one this is a main concern to keep in mind.  

There is going to be a Second Sub-application, used by the actual service advisors so they are able to keep up to date with what's happening through our system. Once a customer has arrived, they will have a generated QR code which will be scanned by the dealer, which will bring up all of the job information for the service advisor for convenience, and will update the customers vehicle status to "Car In Workshop"

It will provide live updates as their car gets worked on. 

(Waiting Customer Arrival -> Car In Workshop -> Is Problems? YES -> Report Problems to Customer NO -> Inform the customer the car is ready to be collected)
